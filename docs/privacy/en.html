Privacy Policy

Nhắc Tốc Độ (Android, package vn.d2o.speedalert) · Effective 19 October 2026

Summary: your location is processed only on your phone and is never sent anywhere. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and collects no personal data.

1. Location data

The app uses precise location (GPS) to work out which road you are driving on, so it can show the speed limit and the upcoming signs and cameras, and play alerts.

  • Location is read only while alerts are switched on, and a "running" notification is always shown then. The app does not request background location access when it is off.
  • All processing happens on the device. Location is never sent to any server, never shared with third parties and never stored as a trip history.

2. Network access

The app connects to the Internet only to download and update its map data (speed limits, road signs), which is hosted on GitHub Releases (github.com, *.githubusercontent.com).

  • A download request contains no location or personal information about you.
  • Like any web server, GitHub may log the IP address and technical details of the request under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.
  • The app always asks before downloading and shows the download size.

3. Permissions

4. Data stored on the device

Your settings and the downloaded map data are kept in the app's private storage, readable only by the app, and are not backed up to the cloud. You can delete the map data under Settings → Dữ liệu → Xoá dữ liệu, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

5. No collection, no sharing

The app has no user accounts, contains no ads, uses no third-party analytics or crash reporting, and does not sell or share data with anyone.

6. Children

The app is meant for drivers and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Safety notice

Information in the app is for reference only and may be out of date. Always obey the actual road signs and do not handle your phone while driving.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, email kenhtaymay@gmail.com.
